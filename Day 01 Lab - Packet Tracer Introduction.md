# DAY 01 LAB - PACKET TRACER INTRODUCTION

## What was conducted in lab?
In this lab, I created two simple LANs that represented two different offices across the world that would connect to one another.
One network is comprised of two PCs connected to a one 2960 switch, connected to a 2911 router, connected to a 5505 firewall, connected to the internet. 
The other network is compromised of two servers connected to one 2960 switch, connected to a 5505 firewall, connected to a 2911 router, connected to the internet.
Finally, a laptop sits between both networks representing a potential attacker connected to the internet.

## Lessons learned?
This lab is meant to act as an introduction to the Packet Tracer application. Nothing was confusing as it was as straight forward as possible requiring no configuration from the user.
